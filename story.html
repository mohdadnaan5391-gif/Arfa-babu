❤️ Our Story ❤️

Arfa, you know our beginning was a little weird. I came to you crying and asking for forgiveness, but what you didn't know was that my whole plan was not just to apologize. Deep inside, I wanted us to be together again.

A lot of things happened after that. There were misunderstandings, times when I unintentionally ignored you, and moments when you felt that I was lying about everything. We both went through many ups and downs, but somehow our story kept moving forward.

The best thing that happened was when we met on my birthday. It was something I had never imagined before. You were sitting right beside me, and I was so, so, so happy that I couldn't even express it properly. I felt relaxed, comfortable, and at peace just because you were there.

When you sat beside me in my car and I got the chance to hold your hand, it became one of my favorite memories. I genuinely couldn't believe it was happening. I kept thinking, "Am I dreaming?" That moment felt unreal in the best way possible.

I also loved buying things for you that day. It wasn't about the things themselves; it was about seeing you there with me and sharing those moments together.

Then you invited me to your home and fed me food, including those famous chawal bina namak wale 😭🫶🏻. Even today, that memory makes me smile. You looked so beautiful, and I felt lucky just being there with you.

❤️ Our Favorite Memories ❤️

One of our favorite memories is eating pani puri and shawarma together. Shawarma became your favorite, and every time I think about it, I remember the fun we had.

Holding your hand for the first time is a memory I will always keep close to my heart. It was such a small moment, but it meant so much to me.

Spending time together in the bank is another memory that I cherish. It may have looked like an ordinary day to everyone else, but for me, every minute spent with you became special.

❤️ My Hope For The Future ❤️

For the future, I hope that no matter what challenges come our way, we continue to stand by each other. I hope you support me during my difficult times, and I promise to keep working hard for our future.

My goal is to build a stable life, earn well, and one day come to ask for your hand with pride and respect. Until then, I hope we continue creating memories, understanding each other better, and supporting one another through every stage of life.

No matter what happens, our story will always be one of the most important parts of my life. ❤️
